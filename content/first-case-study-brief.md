# First case study — notes for Kiara

Rough notes are enough. Write the way you would explain the work to a curious person.

- **Project / organization / year:** What was it called, who was it for, and when?
- **Your role:** What did you personally own? Who else was involved?
- **The people:** Who were you trying to reach? What cultural or community context mattered?
- **The problem:** What was happening, and what needed to change?
- **What wasn't clicking:** What insight changed the way you approached it?
- **What you did:** The decisions, actions, and tradeoffs. What did you choose, and why?
- **What changed:** Verified metrics with timeframe and source, or an honest qualitative result. No metric is better than an invented metric.
- **What you learned:** What would you repeat or change?
- **Work artifacts:** Screenshots, campaign content, event materials, messaging documents, research, or photos you can share. For each, explain what it demonstrates and your contribution.
- **Personal photography:** A portrait or candid that feels like you, plus its context and photographer credit if needed.
- **Project mood:** Colors, imagery, or materials already associated with the work.

Do not include confidential material. If a client cannot be named, describe the context truthfully and make the anonymity clear.
